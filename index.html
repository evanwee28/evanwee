A. About Me

My name is Evan Hector Sy Wee, a student in the Computer Science for Software Development program at York University’s Lassonde School of Engineering, graduating in 2028. My primary career goals are to complete my degree, expand my industry expertise through impactful co-op experiences, and transition into a full time role as a software developer.

Growing up in Canada while traveling extensively throughout my youth shaped my perspective early on. Experiencing diverse cultures and environments taught me to remain open minded, adaptable, and inclusive, avoiding rigid ways of thinking. This global outlook allows me to approach problem solving and collaboration with empathy and dynamic curiosity.

Choosing Lassonde was a natural step because its commitment to empowering creative engineers aligned with my desire to build scalable software solutions. A core strength I bring to my journey at York is my composure under pressure. Whether navigating tight project deadlines or resolving unexpected system issues, I maintain a calm, methodical mindset that prevents panic and delivers steady, reliable results, a quality vital to effective leadership and teamwork.

My greatest source of inspiration is Anthony Bourdain. His profound interest in the world, unvarnished curiosity, and genuine respect for people from all walks of life demonstrated what it means to listen and connect. From him, I learned that impactful leadership requires humility, a willingness to step into the unknown, and an authentic desire to understand others before leading them.
